Pilot Logbook

A free digital pilot logbook for Windows and macOS, laid out like the CASA (Australian) pilot logbook.

Download

Windows 10/11 · macOS 13 Ventura or later

Your logbook's layout

Columns (1)–(16) for single- and multi-engine ICUS, dual, command and co-pilot time, day and night, plus instrument and sim.

Pages and totals

See your flights as logbook pages with totals for this page, brought forward and to date. Running totals are always up to date.

Recency at a glance

Passenger carriage (day and night), night VFR, IFR approaches, flight review and IPC, each with its expiry date.

Quick entry

Pick your aircraft from your own list, enter the total and night time, and the right columns are filled in for you.

Your data stays yours

Your logbook is stored on your own computer, with backup and restore and automatic weekly backups.

Optional Google Sheets backup

Keep a copy in your own Google Drive, updated automatically, and restore it onto a new computer.
