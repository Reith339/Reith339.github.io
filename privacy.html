Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 November 2026

Pilot Logbook is a desktop app for Windows and macOS. This policy explains what data the app handles and where that data goes. In short, your logbook stays on your computer, and in your own Google Drive if you turn on Google Sheets backup. The developer never receives it.

Data stored on your computer

Everything you enter is stored in a database file on your own computer: flights, aircraft, names of pilots and crew, places, remarks, brought-forward totals and settings. The app has no servers, no user accounts, and no analytics or tracking. Your data isn't sent to the developer or to anyone else.

The app also keeps automatic backup copies in the same folder on your computer. Uninstalling the app doesn't delete your logbook; you can remove the folder yourself.

Optional Google Sheets backup

If you choose File → Google Sheets Backup → Connect Google Account, you sign in to Google in your web browser and grant the app limited access to your Google account. The app requests these permissions:

  • See, edit, create and delete only the specific Google Drive files you use with this app (https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file). The app uses this to create a spreadsheet named “Pilot Logbook backup” in your Google Drive, update it with your logbook, find it again, and read it back when you restore. It can't see or change any other files in your Drive.
  • Your email address (openid and email). This is only shown in the app so you can see which Google account is connected.

The app sends your logbook data directly from your computer to Google's servers (Google Sheets and Google Drive APIs) to store it in your own spreadsheet. It isn't sent to the developer or any third party. To keep you signed in, a Google access token is stored in the app's settings on your computer.

Pilot Logbook's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Data from Google is used only to provide the backup and restore feature. It isn't used for advertising, sold, or shared, and isn't read by people.

Turning off Google Sheets backup

  • In the app, choose File → Google Sheets Backup → Disconnect. This removes the stored token from your computer and asks Google to revoke the app's access.
  • You can also remove the app's access at any time from your Google Account at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • The backup spreadsheet belongs to you and stays in your Google Drive until you delete it.

Children

The app isn't directed at children under 13 and doesn't knowingly collect their information.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
